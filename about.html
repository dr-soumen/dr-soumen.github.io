About

Clinical psychiatry with an academic and neuroscience orientation.

Dr. Soumen Karmakar is a Consultant Psychiatrist from Kolkata, currently working at the Institute of Neurosciences Kolkata.

Originally from Murshidabad, he is an alumnus of Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira, Belur Math. He completed undergraduate and postgraduate medical education at Calcutta National Medical College, with psychiatric training including Calcutta Pavlov Hospital.

He cleared the DNB (Psychiatry) examination conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences in 2024. His academic interests include psychopharmacology, forensic psychiatry and consultation-liaison psychiatry, alongside neuropsychiatry and translational neuroscience.
