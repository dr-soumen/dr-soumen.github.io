Research & academic work

From clinical questions to translational neuroscience.

Research interests include psychopharmacology, consultation-liaison psychiatry, neuropsychiatry, biomarkers, clinical trials and neuromodulation.

Academic work

Research papers have been presented at state and national conferences, with publications in national and international journals. Dr. Karmakar has served as a reviewer for journals including BJPsych Open, Scientific Reports and Frontiers in Psychiatry.

He is an Assistant Editor for the Bengal Journal of Psychiatry and has a continuing interest in teaching and medical education.
